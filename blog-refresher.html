Frequently asked

How is this different from Ahrefs or Screaming Frog?+
SEO crawlers like Screaming Frog diagnose issues by dumping spreadsheets of broken links and empty tags. RightAgents takes diagnosis to action: our autonomous agents cross-reference your live inventory catalog, rewrite out-of-date sections, refresh facts, and format the output directly into publication-ready copy for your approval.
Does it publish automatically?+
Never without your sign-off. We operate with a strict Human-In-The-Loop paradigm. You get a clean side-by-side visual diff of all suggested text, product link, and schema adjustments. With one click, approved content is dispatched to your CMS.
Can ChatGPT do this?+
ChatGPT has no visibility into your real-time stock levels, SKU catalogs, discontinued inventory, or CMS architecture. BlogRefresher binds advanced LLM agents to your actual product database, verifying every single recommended item is in stock and accurately priced before publishing.
