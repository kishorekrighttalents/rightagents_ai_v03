Frequently asked

Do we need to clean all our historical data before building agents? +

No. We prioritize an iterative approach, indexing and establishing deterministic validation on active high-priority schemas first, allowing agents to deliver value while historical archives undergo non-destructive background syncing.

How do you handle PII and corporate IP? +

We employ automated PII masking, tokenization, and strict multi-tenant access boundaries within your existing enterprise VPC or air-gapped cloud perimeter before vector ingestion takes place.

Can our team maintain the pipeline after handoff? +

Yes. Everything is built with standard infrastructure-as-code patterns, clean telemetry, and comprehensive architectural runbooks, ensuring full sovereignty for your internal engineering organization.
